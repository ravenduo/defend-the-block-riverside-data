Lombard Street map data

A small, free pilot feed for Defend the Block. Coverage is limited to Lombard Street, California. The app downloads a fixed regional snapshot; it does not send a selected location or search text.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, provided under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). Extract supplied by Geofabrik. The downloadable snapshot is the derived database and includes its acquisition metadata.
